# The Crowd Was Talking: Regulator-Confirmed Financial Misconduct and Its Footprints in Online Communities

This repository is for the paper "The Crowd Was Talking: Regulator-Confirmed Financial Misconduct and Its Footprints in Online Communities" submitted to the Web Conference 2027. It contains:

- 381 cases of misconduct confirmed by the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) in 2020 and 2021
- 2,000 randomly drawn reference stocks
- the Reddit IDs of all posts and comments that mention these stocks, with their LLM labels
- the code that computes the monthly signals

## Contents

```
data/
  cases.csv              381 cases
  reference_stocks.csv   2,000 randomly drawn SEC stocks
  mentions.csv.gz        one row per (post or comment, stock)
  daily_activity.csv     all posts and comments per day in the 13 subreddits
  signals_monthly.csv    monthly signals, generated by code/signals.py
code/
  signals.py             computes data/signals_monthly.csv from the other files
```

## Data

### cases.csv

One row per case. A case is one company whose misconduct period overlaps 2020–2021 and whose stock could be linked to discussions.

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| `case_id` | Case identifier, also used as `stock_id` in `mentions.csv.gz` |
| `sec_documents` | SEC documents of the case (litigation releases, administrative orders, trading suspensions), separated by `;` |
| `document_url` | URL of the SEC document |
| `company`, `cik`, `tickers` | Company name, SEC Central Index Key, tickers stated in the documents |
| `sec_classification` | Program area in the SEC's annual enforcement reports. Trading suspensions: `Trading Suspension` |
| `type` | Grouped type: `misstatement` (issuer reporting / audit & accounting), `manipulation`, `insider trading`, `offering`, `suspension`, `other` |
| `misconduct_start`, `misconduct_end` | First and last month of the misconduct (`YYYY-MM`), as stated in the documents. For trading suspensions, the end is the month of the suspension |
| `misconduct_months` | Length of the misconduct period in months |
| `first_sec_action` | Date of the SEC's first public action against the company (earliest trading suspension, litigation release, or administrative order) |

### reference_stocks.csv

2,000 tickers drawn at random from the SEC's list of company tickers (`company_tickers.json`).

### mentions.csv.gz

One row per (post or comment, stock) from October 2019 to March 2022. A post or comment that mentions several stocks appears once per stock.

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| `reddit_id` | Reddit fullname: `t3_` for posts, `t1_` for comments |
| `kind` | `post` or `comment` |
| `subreddit` | Subreddit |
| `created_utc` | Creation time (Unix seconds, UTC) |
| `stock_id` | `case_id` of a case, or `control:<ticker>` for a reference stock |
| `group` | `case` or `reference` |
| `author_idx` | Pseudonymous author index, consistent across rows. Empty if the account was deleted |
| `duplicate` | 1 if another account wrote the same or a nearly identical text (exact normalized hash or MinHash) about the same stock within one hour. Texts naming 10 or more tracked stocks and bot accounts are not counted |
| `stance` | Stance toward the stock: `bullish`, `bearish`, `neutral` |
| `allegation` | Allegation of misconduct around the stock: `manipulation` (fraud, scam, pump and dump, manipulation, or an SEC action cited as a sign of wrongdoing), `insider` (insider trading), `both`, `none` |

Matching uses titles and bodies of posts and the bodies of comments. Posts and comments whose text was `[deleted]` or `[removed]` at the time of archiving are excluded. Posts whose body was removed but whose title remains are kept and matched on the title.

The texts can be retrieved by `reddit_id` from the Reddit API or from the Pushshift archives.

### daily_activity.csv

Number of posts, comments, and both together per day across the 13 subreddits, excluding deleted and removed texts. This is the denominator for volume and for the surge model.

### Subreddits and the full corpus

The data cover 13 subreddits: wallstreetbets, Wallstreetbetsnew, WallStreetbetsELITE, smallstreetbets, pennystocks, RobinHoodPennyStocks, SPACs, Shortsqueeze, options, stocks, investing, StockMarket, Daytrading.

The full set of posts and comments, including those that mention no case or reference stock, is not part of this release. It can be obtained from the monthly Pushshift dumps of Reddit submissions and comments (`RS_YYYY-MM.zst` and `RC_YYYY-MM.zst`), which are distributed through Academic Torrents <!-- TODO: link to the torrent used -->. To rebuild the corpus used here:

1. Keep the 13 subreddits above.
2. Keep posts and comments created from 1 October 2019 to 31 March 2022 (UTC).
3. Drop items whose text is empty, `[deleted]`, or `[removed]`. For posts, the text is the title and the body together, so a post whose body was removed but whose title remains is kept.

This yields 3,016,115 posts and 57,306,836 comments, which matches the daily totals in `daily_activity.csv`.

## Signals

`code/signals.py` reads `data/` and writes `data/signals_monthly.csv`, with one row per stock-month in which the stock is mentioned at least once. For cases, only mentions before the SEC's first public action count.

| Signal | Definition |
|---|---|
| `volume` | Mentions divided by all posts and comments of the month |
| `surge` | Share of the month's days in the surge state of an online two-state burst detection model (Kleinberg, 2002), fitted to the stock's daily share of all posts and comments. Surge rate: 2× the usual share. Usual share: estimated from the preceding 90 days. Entry cost: 1 × ln(number of days). Each day's state depends only on that day and earlier days |
| `reemergence` | Days without any mention before the month's first mention, counted from the start of the data if the stock had not been mentioned before |
| `newcomers` | Share of the month's authors who mention the stock for the first time in the data. Not computed for the first 3 months of the data |
| `coordination` | Share of the month's mentions flagged as `duplicate` |
| `allegations` | Share of the month's labelled mentions with an allegation (`manipulation`, `insider`, or `both`) |
| `bullishness` | (bullish − bearish) / (bullish + bearish) among the month's labelled mentions, 0 if there is no bullish or bearish mention |

Run:

```
cd code
python signals.py
```

The script needs Python 3 with numpy and pandas and takes about a minute.
